# ROB 599: Physically-Grounded AI Agents

University of Michigan, Fall 2026.

Physical AI is the study of agents that perceive, act and learn in the real world. This
course reads the field through the [paper hub](https://yayuanli-org.github.io/awesome-physical-ai/),
a database of the papers with a page on top, and argues about it. Two lectures set the
frame. Every session after that is a debate on two papers. One side says the paper's
claim holds, the other says it does not. The room settles what the claim is, whether the
evaluation substantiates it, and whether the method is the right way. Papers are read
with [PACES](https://medium.com/@jasoncorso/how-to-read-conference-papers-fa78c75f78aa):
problem, approach, claims, evaluation, substantiation.
